How to create a Discord bot
  1. Go to the Discord Developer Portal (discord.com/developers/applications)
  2. Click New Application, give it a name, and click Create
  3. Go to the Bot tab on the left sidebar
  4. Your bot token is shown once after creation — click Copy to copy it and paste it below. If you missed it, click Reset Token to generate a new one
  5. Scroll down and enable Privileged Gateway Intents:
    • SERVER MEMBERS INTENT
    • MESSAGE CONTENT INTENT
  6. Go to OAuth2 > URL Generator on the left sidebar
  7. Under Scopes, check bot
  8. Under Bot Permissions, check Connect and Speak
  9. Copy the generated URL at the bottom and open it in your browser to invite the bot to your server
Advanced: Override bundled tools

yt-dlp and FFmpeg are bundled with the app. Only change these if you want to use a different version.
